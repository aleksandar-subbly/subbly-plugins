# Web3Forms: customer guide

Share these sections with the user in plain words when they ask how the integration works. Quote the prompts as written, so the user can copy them.

## What it does

Your website forms send each submission to Web3Forms, and Web3Forms emails it to you. You don't need a server or any code. Subbly Builder builds and connects the forms for you.

## Get your Form Access Key

1. Go to https://web3forms.com/.
2. Create a free account and generate a new form to get an Access Key.
3. Confirm the verification email Web3Forms sends you.
4. Copy the Access Key. It looks like `xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx`.

Web3Forms never needs your password, only this key.

## Configure the plugin

Paste the key into the **Form Access Key** field when you install the Web3Forms plugin, or later in the plugin's settings. You enter it once, and every form on your site uses it.

## Where your emails go

Submissions go to the email address on your Web3Forms account. To change it, change it in Web3Forms. Subbly doesn't store a recipient address.

## Test a form

Open the page in the preview or on your live site, fill in the form and send it. You should see a success message on the page, and the email should arrive within a minute or two. Check spam and Promotions the first time. Each test sends a real email. Web3Forms briefly blocks you if you send many in a row.

## Common problems

- **The form says it is unavailable:** the Access Key is missing or wrong. Re-enter it in the plugin settings.
- **The form says it sent, but no email arrived:** check spam and Promotions, and check the email address on your Web3Forms account.
- **It works in the preview but not on your live domain:** if you turned on domain restriction in Web3Forms, add your live domain there. Otherwise contact Web3Forms support.
- **"Too many messages":** wait about an hour, then try again.
